What typingname is

Every name is generated by an AI model and scored using an automated, heuristic-based system (length, pronounceability, memorability, distinctiveness). Domain availability, and pricing shown in the app, can change between the moment you view it and the moment you attempt to register — always confirm both on the registrar's site before completing a purchase.
